Input 1 Sig/Clip

-∞ dBFS
Front Instrument jack with the source button out (orange band showing), or rear XLR/TRS with it pushed in. Gain, the pull-out −20 dB pad and Soft Limit are analog: set them on the Mbox and watch this meter.

Input 2 Sig/Clip

-∞ dBFS
Rear inputs line up with their front controls (Input 2 is the far-left combo jack seen from behind). Aim for peaks around −12 dBFS; red means the converter is clipping, lower the gain.

+48V Phantom Power

Phantom power is switched only by the +48V button on the Mbox (press and hold for the soft-button hold time). Its LED is the only indicator: no software, including Avid's, can switch or read it. It feeds both XLR inputs, max 4 mA per mic.

Condenser mics need it; dynamics are unaffected. With ribbon mics, turn it off and wait 10 s before connecting or disconnecting.
