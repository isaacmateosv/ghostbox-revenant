Input 1

−∞ dBFS

Front Instrument jack with the source button out, or the rear XLR/TRS combo jack with it pushed in. Gain, the −20 dB pad and Soft Limit are analog: set them on the Mbox and watch this meter. Aim for peaks around −12 dBFS.

Input 2

−∞ dBFS

Seen from behind, the rear jacks line up with their front controls (Input 2 is the far-left combo jack). Red means the converter is clipping: lower the gain.

+48V phantom power

Switched only by the +48V button on the Mbox (hold it for the soft-button hold time). Its LED is the only indicator: no software, Avid's included, can switch or read it. It feeds both XLR inputs.

Condenser mics need it; dynamic mics don't mind it. With ribbon mics, switch it off and wait 10 s before plugging or unplugging.

Device

1. Teach it the button

The Multi button talks to the computer over the Mbox's Control MIDI port. Click Learn, then press Multi on the Mbox once.

2. Choose what it does

Play/Pause, Space Bar and Record send key presses (macOS asks once for Accessibility permission). Forward to DAW resends the message on a virtual MIDI port called Mbox 3 Multi Button: map it with your DAW's MIDI learn.

Soft button hold time

How long to hold the soft buttons (Multi, +48V, Mono, Dim). Last Multi action: none
